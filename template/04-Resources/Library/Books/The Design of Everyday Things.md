# The Design of Everyday Things

Re-read for the third time. Still the clearest argument for designing around how people actually behave.

## Ideas I keep coming back to
- **Affordances vs signifiers**: the affordance is what's possible, the signifier is what tells you.
- The gulfs of execution and evaluation. Most bad UI I see at work is a wide gulf of evaluation.
- "Norman doors" are everywhere once you notice them. The office kitchen has two.
