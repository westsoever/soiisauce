# Weekly planning

Sunday evening, 20 minutes.

1. Look at last week's [[weekly-2026-10-11|weekly review]].
2. Pick three things that matter. Not ten.
3. Block two mornings for deep work ([[Maker's Schedule, Manager's Schedule]]).
4. Check People for anyone I owe a call.

From [[Four Thousand Weeks]]: you will never get everything done. Choose.
