# New project - yes or no

1. Is it something I chose or that excites me? If not, no.
2. What does it cost a week? A project is about 15% (Capacity → A new project?).
3. Does it fit under the 80% line? Yes: say yes.
4. Over 80 but under 100: only with an end date, and something paused until then.
5. Over 100: no, or finish, pause or drop something first.
